About The Pelican

Rooftop Restaurant and Bar · Port Harcourt

Elevated dining in Eliozu

The Pelican Rooftop Restaurant and Bar brings together rooftop seating, a full kitchen, and a bar known for great cocktails. Whether you are here for swallow and soup, a seafood platter, grilled plates, or a long evening of drinks, the space is built for unhurried nights above the city.

Find us on Gas Turbine Road, Eneka Link Road, off New Eliozu — open through to 11:59 pm.
